# WHO Guidance Notes

source: WHO
url: https://www.who.int/health-topics/noncommunicable-diseases

WHO guidance emphasizes prevention of noncommunicable disease risk with healthy diet, physical activity, reduced tobacco and alcohol use, and regular screening for metabolic risk factors.

source: WHO
url: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diabetes

WHO highlights diabetes screening and follow-up when blood glucose is elevated. Lifestyle modification and early clinician review are important for risk reduction.

source: WHO
url: https://www.who.int/health-topics/liver-disease

WHO public-health material notes that persistent abnormal liver markers should be evaluated by clinicians and interpreted with patient history.
