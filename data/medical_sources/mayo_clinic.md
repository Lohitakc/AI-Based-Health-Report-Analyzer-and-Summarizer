# Mayo Clinic Guidance Notes

source: Mayo Clinic
url: https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/complete-blood-count/about/pac-20384919

Mayo Clinic describes CBC components including hemoglobin, red blood cells, white blood cells, platelets, MCV, and MCH. Abnormal patterns can suggest anemia, infection, inflammation, bleeding risk, or marrow-related issues and should be interpreted clinically.

source: Mayo Clinic
url: https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/liver-function-tests/about/pac-20394595

Mayo Clinic explains LFT interpretation through bilirubin, AST, ALT, ALP, and related enzymes. Persistent elevation can suggest liver or bile-duct stress and requires clinician review.

source: Mayo Clinic
url: https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/blood-urea-nitrogen/about/pac-20384921

Mayo Clinic notes that BUN and creatinine are commonly used to assess kidney function and hydration status together with clinical context.
