# CDC Guidance Notes

source: CDC
url: https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/diabetes-testing/index.html

CDC describes common glucose testing thresholds:
- Fasting plasma glucose: normal below 100 mg/dL, prediabetes 100 to 125 mg/dL, diabetes at or above 126 mg/dL on repeat testing.
- Random plasma glucose may support diabetes diagnosis when markedly elevated with symptoms.

source: CDC

CDC cardiovascular prevention material focuses on LDL, HDL, triglycerides, blood pressure, body weight, physical activity, and smoking cessation as key risk elements.

source: CDC
url: https://www.cdc.gov/heart-disease/about/index.html

Raised lipids and glucose should be interpreted with clinical risk factors and longitudinal follow-up.
